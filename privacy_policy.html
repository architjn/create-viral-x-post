Privacy Policy for Viral X Post Helper

Last Updated: 11/04/2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how the "Viral X Post Helper" Chrome extension ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our service. Our commitment is to respect your privacy regarding any information we might handle while operating the extension.

2. Data Collected

The core function of this extension involves analyzing text you provide. Therefore, we process the following data:

  • Post Text: The text content that you input into the extension's interface for analysis and enhancement suggestions.

We do not collect any other personally identifiable information (PII) like your name, email address, IP address, or browsing history unrelated to the extension's direct function.

3. Use of Data

The text you provide is used solely for the purpose of generating virality enhancement suggestions. This involves:

  • Sending the text to an external Application Programming Interface (API) provided by Dify.
  • Receiving the analysis and suggestions back from the API.
  • Displaying these suggestions to you within the extension's interface.

The text is processed ephemerally by the external API provider according to their terms and privacy policy (which you should review).

4. Data Storage

To facilitate the asynchronous operation between the popup and the background processing, we use the browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your computer to temporarily store:

  • The input text while it awaits processing or is being processed.
  • The generated suggestions received from the API.
  • The current operational state of the extension (e.g., "processing", "idle", "error").
  • Any error messages generated during the process.

This data resides only on your local machine within the browser's secure storage area for the extension. It is automatically cleared when the suggestions are displayed, an error occurs and is displayed, or typically when the popup window is closed. We do not store this data on any servers we control.

5. Data Sharing

We share the text you input exclusively with our chosen external API provider: Dify. This sharing is essential for the core functionality of providing analysis and suggestions. We do not sell or share your input text or the generated suggestions with any other third parties.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of Dify to understand their data handling practices.

6. Security

We utilize the standard security mechanisms provided by the Chrome Extension platform and `chrome.storage.local` to handle your data. Communication with the external API is typically secured using HTTPS. However, please be aware that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. User Rights and Control

You have control over the data handled by this extension:

  • You can view and clear the data stored by the extension using your browser's developer tools or specific extension management features.
  • You can stop using the extension at any time.
  • You can uninstall the extension, which will remove it and its associated local storage from your browser.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will indicate the date of the last update at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy occasionally to stay informed.
